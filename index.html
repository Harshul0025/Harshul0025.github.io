01 // about

I like projects that actually do something.

I'm a B.Tech CSE student (2024–2028) at BML Munjal University, Gurugram. My first big build was an obstacle-avoiding car in school, which won the MANAK INSPIRE Award. Since then I've moved from Arduino to AI agents, but the goal is the same: build things people can see working.

Lately that means a game that cleans your Gmail, slides you control with your hands, and a disaster-response platform with flood and fire prediction. Off the keyboard, I lead outreach at the ACM BMU Student Chapter and help run hackathons.
